Every valve decision comes from a Verus-verified controller running on your machine, and the same code can run inside a Nanvix micro-VM. Soil moisture is simulated by the bridge.

Connecting to local bridge…

Connect your workspace

This page talks to a bridge on 127.0.0.1:8787 that runs the controller and the Verus toolchain. Start it from the repo:

cd conduit-lab
python3 bridge/conduit_bridge.py --nanvix-host <user>@<windows-host>

Already running? Chrome asks once to let this site access devices on your local network. Choose Allow, or re-enable it under the site settings (the icon left of the address bar), then reload.

Every control stays disabled until the bridge answers. Nothing on this page works without it.
